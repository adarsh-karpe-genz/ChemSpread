Process 01

The Haber Process

The synthesis of ammonia from nitrogen and hydrogen is one of the most important industrial processes ever developed. It enabled the mass production of fertilisers, supporting global food supply.

N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g) ΔH = −92 kJ/mol

The reaction is exothermic and reversible. A compromise between yield and rate is achieved at 450°C — low enough for reasonable equilibrium yield, high enough for acceptable rate.

Operating Conditions
Temperature450°C
Pressure150–300 atm
CatalystIron (Fe) with K₂O/Al₂O₃ promoters
Yield~15% per pass (recycled)
FeedstocksN₂ (from air), H₂ (from natural gas)
AgricultureFertilisersGlobal Food SupplyExplosives
Process 02

The Contact Process

The industrial manufacture of sulphuric acid — the world's most produced chemical by mass. Essential for fertilisers, detergents, dyes, and batteries.

S + O₂ → SO₂
2SO₂ + O₂ ⇌ 2SO₃ (V₂O₅ catalyst)
SO₃ + H₂SO₄ → H₂S₂O₇ → H₂SO₄
Operating Conditions
Temperature450°C (compromise)
Pressure1–2 atm (near atmospheric)
CatalystVanadium pentoxide (V₂O₅)
Conversion>99.5% (double absorption)
FertilisersDetergentsBatteriesPharmaceuticals
Process 03

The Chlor-Alkali Process

Electrolysis of brine (sodium chloride solution) to produce three important industrial chemicals: chlorine, hydrogen, and sodium hydroxide.

2NaCl(aq) + 2H₂O → Cl₂ + H₂ + 2NaOH

Process 04

Petroleum Refining

Crude oil is separated by fractional distillation — different hydrocarbons condense at different temperatures as vapour rises up the column. Each fraction has characteristic properties and uses.

Refinery Gas
below 20°C

C₁–C₄. Methane, ethane, propane, butane. Used as fuel and chemical feedstocks.

Petrol (Gasoline)
20–70°C

C₄–C₁₂. Motor fuel. High demand fraction; further processed by cracking.

Naphtha / Kerosene
70–200°C

C₉–C₁₆. Aviation fuel (kerosene) and chemical feedstock for plastics.

Diesel / Gas Oil
200–350°C

C₁₅–C₂₅. Road and rail transport fuel. Also used in central heating.

Fuel Oil
350–500°C

C₂₀–C₇₀. Ships, power stations. Thick, high viscosity fraction.

Bitumen / Residue
above 500°C

C₇₀+. Road surfacing, roofing. Remains after all lighter fractions distilled off.
